# GENERAL QUESTIONS

## What is abso.?
abso. is the games practice of ABSOLUTEARTT&reg;.
We make small games, strange ideas, playful mechanics and tiny worlds.
No particular genre. No particular style. Just ideas worth playing with.

## Are the games free?
Yes.
Our games are designed to be free to play.
Some games may contain advertising or optional purchases, but the core experience remains playable without paying.

## Do I need an account to play?
Not necessarily.
You can play our games without creating an account where the game allows it.
Some features may require an account, such as saving your profile, scores, collections or other abso. features.

## What is the Dot?
You'll probably notice him.
The Dot is a little character that appears across the abso. universe.
Sometimes he's the player. Sometimes he's a companion. Sometimes he's the problem.
He doesn't have one fixed form or one fixed story.
Same soul. Different life.

## Who makes abso.?
abso. is the games practice of ABSOLUTEARTT&reg;, an independent creative studio.
We make films, games, music, artwork and other creative experiments.
abso. exists for the games.

## Can I play the games on mobile?
Where a game is released for mobile, yes.
Some games may also be available on the web or other platforms.
The availability of each game is shown on its individual game page.

## Do you make games for brands?
We can.
abso. is primarily an authored games practice, but we also develop original game concepts and interactive experiences for selected collaborations.
We don't make generic games just to fill a brief.
The idea comes first.

## I found a bug. Where do I report it?
Please email us at:
[games@absoluteartt.com](mailto:games@absoluteartt.com)
Tell us the name of the game, what happened, and the device or browser you were using.
Screenshots are always helpful.

## I have an idea for a game.
Good.
We like strange ideas.
You can send us a note at:
[games@absoluteartt.com](mailto:games@absoluteartt.com)
We can't promise we'll make it, but we'll read it.
