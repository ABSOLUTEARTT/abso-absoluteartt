# PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 2027

ABSOLUTEARTT ENTERTAINMENT LLP ("ABSOLUTEARTT", "we", "our", or "us") operates abso., our games practice.
We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information reasonably necessary to operate our games, website and related services.
This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected and how we use it.

## 1. Information We Collect
Depending on how you use abso., we may collect limited information such as:
* Information you provide when contacting us.
* Account information where an account is required.
* Game scores, progress, achievements or other gameplay information associated with your account.
* Technical information necessary to operate and improve our games and website.
* Information required for security, fraud prevention and troubleshooting.

We do not ask you to provide personal information that we do not reasonably need.

## 2. Information You Provide
If you contact us by email or another communication channel, we may retain the information you provide so that we can respond to you and maintain a record of the communication.

## 3. Game and Technical Information
Our games and website may automatically receive limited technical information such as:
* Device type
* Operating system
* Browser information
* Approximate location derived from technical information
* Diagnostic information
* Gameplay or performance information

This information may be used to operate, maintain, secure and improve our services.

## 4. Accounts
Where an abso. account is available, we may use information associated with the account to provide features such as:
* Profile management
* Scores and leaderboards
* Game progress
* Achievements
* Collections
* Other abso. account features

We do not sell personal information.

## 5. Advertising and Third-Party Services
Some free games may contain advertising or use third-party services.
Those services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.
Where third-party services are used, we recommend reviewing their applicable privacy policies to understand how they process information.

## 6. Cookies and Similar Technologies
Our website or third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies for functionality, security, analytics or other legitimate purposes.
You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings.

## 7. Children
Our games are intended for general audiences unless a particular game states otherwise.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.
If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

## 8. Data Security
We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.
No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Data Retention
We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including legal, security and operational requirements.

## 10. Your Rights
Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to access, correct or request deletion of certain information.
To make a privacy-related request, contact:
[privacy@absoluteartt.com](mailto:privacy@absoluteartt.com)

## 11. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page.

## 12. Contact
For privacy questions or requests:
